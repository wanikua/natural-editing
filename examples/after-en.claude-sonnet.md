# Fennel 2.0 is out

Fennel 2.0 shipped on March 3, after 14 months of work. It's a note-taking app for writers, researchers and lifelong learners.

Search is four times faster on libraries over 10,000 notes, and results come back in under 50 ms. We rebuilt offline mode from the ground up, so you can open your notes without a connection. End-to-end encryption is on by default. Over 12,000 people in 40 countries use Fennel.

After you upgrade, you may need to reconfigure some features.
